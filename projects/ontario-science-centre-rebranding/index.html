Ontario Science Centre Rebranding & IMC

Overview of a rebranding and integrated marketing communications project created for the Ontario Science Centre.

View Full Google Doc ↗All Projects

Overview

This academic strategy project explored how the Ontario Science Centre could refresh its brand and reconnect with modern audiences through a stronger integrated marketing approach. The work combined repositioning, audience understanding and channel planning into one cohesive direction.

My Role

I developed the rebranding logic, campaign direction and IMC framework, covering identity thinking, PR, digital content, social media, OOH and SEO/SEM recommendations.

Outcome

The final output demonstrated a practical brand-refresh direction with a connected communication plan designed to improve relevance, visibility and audience engagement.
