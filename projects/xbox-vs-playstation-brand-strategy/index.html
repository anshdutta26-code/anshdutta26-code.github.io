Xbox vs PlayStation Brand Strategy

Overview of a comparative brand strategy study examining Xbox and PlayStation.

View Full Google Doc ↗All Projects

Overview

This project compared two major gaming brands to understand how they differ across positioning, target audiences, messaging, digital presence and competitive strategy. The goal was to translate those differences into useful strategic insight.

My Role

I analysed both brands across audience fit, communication approach, ecosystem strength and market positioning, then distilled the findings into a concise strategic comparison.

Outcome

The final output highlighted where each brand was stronger, how they approached their audiences differently and what broader lessons could be drawn from their competitive strategies.
