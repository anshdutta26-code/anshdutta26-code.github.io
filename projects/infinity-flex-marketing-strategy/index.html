Overview

This project involved building a complete growth strategy for Infinity Flex using market understanding, customer segmentation and structured marketing planning. The intention was to create a usable roadmap rather than a purely theoretical report.

My Role

I worked on STP, the 4Ps, competitor analysis, channel strategy and KPI thinking so the brand could have a clearer direction across positioning, communication and performance measurement.

Outcome

The final strategy presented a focused marketing roadmap with stronger audience clarity, sharper positioning and clearer growth priorities.
