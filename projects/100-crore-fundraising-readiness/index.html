₹100 Cr Fundraising Readiness

Case study covering forecasting, P&L interpretation, commercial assumptions and investor-readiness work for a proposed ₹100 crore Swiss Military fundraising initiative.

Project Type

Professional Strategic Finance Collaboration Project

Primary Focus

Fundraising preparation, forecasting, P&L interpretation, commercial assumptions, investor storytelling and Legal/Finance coordination

Project Snapshot

Scale: Proposed ₹100 crore fundraising exercise. Stakeholders: CFO, Finance, Legal and management stakeholders. Outcome: Investor-readiness work completed; the fundraising initiative was later shelved before execution.

Project Context

I had the opportunity to work alongside the CFO, Finance team and Legal team during preparation for a proposed ₹100 crore fundraising exercise at Swiss Military. The work sat at the intersection of business planning, market understanding and financial storytelling. A credible capital story had to connect what the business had done historically, where future growth was expected to come from, which operating assumptions supported that growth and how those assumptions translated into financial projections.

The Objective

The objective was to support a credible, review-ready business case for external capital. That meant going beyond an ambitious headline number. Historical performance, forward-looking assumptions, P&L logic, category/market expansion, forecast drivers and supporting documentation had to tell one coherent story that could withstand internal review and potential investor questions.

My Role & Ownership

I worked cross-functionally with senior finance and legal stakeholders, contributing primarily to the commercial assumptions, forecast narrative and presentation layer. I was not the financial signatory or legal advisor. My role was to help organize business inputs, connect market/category plans to forward-looking projections, make complex financial information easier to communicate, and keep the commercial story aligned with the numbers being reviewed by Finance.

What I Worked On / Built

  • Worked with CFO/Finance stakeholders on forecast inputs and the growth assumptions underlying the proposed raise.
  • Supported the presentation and interpretation of P&L and broader financial information so the commercial and financial narratives remained aligned.
  • Connected market expansion, category-growth plans and operating assumptions to forward-looking projections.
  • Helped structure management-review information with emphasis on clarity, consistency and the logic behind the numbers.
  • Coordinated with Legal and Finance where supporting information, clarification or documentation was required.
  • Reviewed the story from an external audience perspective: what the business is, how it grows, what assumptions support that growth and why external capital would be relevant.
  • Contributed to investor-oriented data decks / presentation material while keeping financial ownership with the appropriate Finance stakeholders.

How I Approached It

The project reinforced that fundraising material cannot be produced in functional silos. Commercial and marketing plans influence revenue expectations; Finance validates model logic and financial statements; Legal supports the process and documentation. I approached my contribution as a connector between these perspectives—making sure commercial assumptions could be explained, challenged and translated into a coherent narrative rather than presented as isolated growth claims.

Impact & Outcome

  • Supported preparation for a high-stakes ₹100 crore proposed fundraising initiative.
  • Gained direct exposure to senior-level financial planning, forecast construction, P&L interpretation and investor-oriented communication.
  • Helped make commercial assumptions and growth plans easier to understand within a financial narrative.
  • Improved the structure and consistency of information prepared for management review by connecting market logic to financial projections.
  • Built practical experience working across CFO/Finance/Legal stakeholders on one decision-making process.

What Happened

The fundraising initiative was later shelved before execution. I therefore do not present this project as a successful ₹100 crore raise. Its value is the work performed during readiness: understanding how operating assumptions become forecasts, how a P&L is interpreted in a growth context, how senior stakeholders challenge a capital story, and how commercial and financial information needs to align before an external fundraising process can proceed.

Key Challenges & Decisions

  • Maintaining consistency between ambitious growth plans and finance-validated assumptions.
  • Presenting complex data simply without oversimplifying the underlying logic.
  • Separating my contribution to business planning/presentation from formal finance or legal ownership.
  • Working with sensitive information that cannot be published in a public portfolio without sanitization.
  • Building a narrative that could answer 'where will growth come from?' rather than merely displaying forecast numbers.

Capabilities Demonstrated
